### Section Completion

```

Privacy:  ✅ = Always (defaults set)
Contact:  ✅ = At least 1 social link added

Progress = (completed / total) * 100
```

## 💡 What We Learned

1. **First impressions are EVERYTHING**
   - Users decide in 3 seconds
   - Beauty = Trust
   - Trust = Engagement

2. **Progress indicators drive action**
   - Seeing 75% complete makes you finish
   - Checkmarks are satisfying
   - Visual feedback is powerful

3. **Micro-interactions matter**
   - Small details create delight
   - Smooth animations feel premium
   - Every hover should reward

4. **Mobile-first prevents problems**
   - Design for small screens first
   - Scale up, not down
   - Touch targets must be adequate

5. **Accessibility is non-negotiable**
   - High contrast helps everyone
   - Keyboard nav is essential
   - Screen readers matter
